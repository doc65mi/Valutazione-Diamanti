Who can sell to us

We buy directly from private sellers. Whether you have inherited a piece of jewellery, wish to sell a loose stone or are considering selling a vintage piece, you can start with a free, no-obligation enquiry.

What to prepare

Send clear photographs and a GIA, HRD or IGI report if you have one. We also consider diamonds without a report. Proof of identity is required if you decide to sell to us.

How we assess your item

We assess carat weight, colour, clarity and cut, alongside fluorescence, proportions, grading reports and the condition of the jewellery. For pieces with several stones, we collect additional details. An initial enquiry does not replace an in-person examination.

What affects our offer

Two stones of the same weight can have different values. Gemmological quality, any treatments, documentation and the item's characteristics all matter. We make our offer after examining the item.

Your appointment and payment

If needed, we arrange an appointment at Via Vincenzo Monti 47 in Milan to examine your item. If you accept our offer, we pay by bank transfer.

We make our own offer

We do not auction your diamond or offer it to a network of buyers. If it meets our requirements, we make you an offer ourselves. One point of contact for a straightforward process.

What we do not buy

We also assess rubies, sapphires and emeralds weighing less than two carats. Our form includes tailored questions for multi-stone jewellery.

—Diamonds weighing less than one carat per stone
—Synthetic or laboratory-grown diamonds
—Diamonds accompanied by AIG, GRA, GWLAB or ALGT reports
—Watch brands other than those we buy: Rolex, Patek Philippe, Audemars Piguet, Cartier, Vacheron Constantin, Richard Mille, Piaget and Jacob & Co
