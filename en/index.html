Our main requirements

We assess natural diamonds of one carat or more per stone, with a GIA, HRD or IGI report, or without a report. We do not buy laboratory-grown diamonds or diamonds with AIG, GRA, GWLAB or ALGT reports. We buy selected watch brands and also assess rubies, sapphires and emeralds weighing less than two carats.

Contact a specialist diamond buyer. La Diamanteria has been buying directly from private sellers in Milan since 2009. We assess each stone using gemmological criteria and, if it meets our requirements, make our own offer, with no auctions or intermediaries.

We assess natural diamonds of one carat or more per stone, with a GIA, HRD or IGI report, or without a report. We do not buy synthetic diamonds or diamonds with AIG, GRA, GWLAB or ALGT reports. We also assess rubies, sapphires and emeralds under two carats.

For an immediate valuation, message us on WhatsApp or call +39 02 466891. Alternatively, complete our online enquiry form. A gemmologist will review your request; the final valuation is made after examining the stone at our showroom.

A diamond's price depends on the 4Cs: carat weight, colour, clarity and cut. Fluorescence, proportions and an internationally recognised grading report also matter. We refer to current market prices in Antwerp, Tel Aviv, New York, Dubai and Mumbai.
