Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Controller and contact details

The data controller is La Diamanteria, based at Via Vincenzo Monti 47, 20123 Milano. VAT / tax identification number: . For information about your personal data or to exercise your rights, contact sl@ladiamanteria.com.

Data we process and why

When you submit a valuation enquiry, we process your name, contact details, preferred contact method, item details and any photographs or grading reports you choose to attach. We use this information to assess your enquiry, respond to you and manage follow-up communications. We do not request identity documents through this form. Our systems also process technical data needed for security, such as IP addresses, sessions and login attempts.

Legal basis and providing your data

We process enquiries to take pre-contractual steps at your request (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Security processing is based on our legitimate interest in protecting the website and preventing abuse (Article 6(1)(f)). Non-essential analytics and advertising tracking are enabled only with your consent (Article 6(1)(a)). The requested contact details are necessary for us to respond; attachments are optional. Acknowledging this privacy information in the form does not authorise marketing activities.

Retention

Enquiries and their attachments are retained for up to 12 months after submission and then deleted from the system. Cleanup runs when new enquiries are received, when the admin inbox is accessed and through the maintenance command. If a sale is completed, contractual records and statutory obligations are handled separately. Cookie preferences are remembered for six months; an admin session expires after 30 minutes of inactivity. External tools have retention periods determined by their settings and providers’ policies.

Access to data and service providers

Enquiries and attachments are accessible to authorised administrators. Hosting and maintenance providers may process data to operate and protect the service. If enabled by the controller, Google provides reCAPTCHA, Analytics, Ads and Tag Manager; Meta provides the Pixel. Analytics and advertising services are loaded only when the relevant cookie preferences allow them. Website fonts may be provided by Google Fonts, which receives technical connection data. External links, including WhatsApp, lead to services with their own privacy policies.

reCAPTCHA and international transfers

When enabled, reCAPTCHA v3 runs when the form is submitted to help prevent automated abuse. Google analyses technical signals to assign a risk score, which our server verifies. If verification fails, the enquiry is not saved; you can contact us by email or telephone instead. Google and Meta services may involve processing outside the European Economic Area under the applicable safeguards and terms of each provider. Please consult their privacy policies for current details.

Your rights

Where applicable under the GDPR, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability or object to processing. You can withdraw tracking consent at any time using the “Cookie preferences” button, without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal. For other requests, contact sl@ladiamanteria.com. You may lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority or another competent supervisory authority.
