About us

La Diamanteria's direct buying service

Valutazione Diamanti helps private sellers have their diamonds and jewellery valued and sold. The service is run by La Diamanteria, established in Milan in 2009. The Silvera family spans five generations of Diamantaires. The family's roots in the diamond trade go back more than 300 years, as documented in historical studies and articles.

Our expertise today

Today, the Silvera brothers buy diamonds directly from private sellers. A gemmologist reviews each enquiry and the stone's characteristics. We do not send items to auction or seek buyers on your behalf.

Our documented history

Francesca Trivellato's book Il commercio interculturale, a study published in Quaderni storici and a newspaper article recount episodes from the family's trading history.

Our Milan boutique

Since 2009, we have welcomed clients by appointment at Via Vincenzo Monti 47 in Milan. Here we can examine your item in person and make you an offer directly. If you accept, we pay by bank transfer.

Valuations and expert reports

We value more than €200 million worth of diamonds each year. Since 2015, our gemmologists, accredited by the Chamber of Commerce, have also prepared sworn valuations for estates, asset division, legal proceedings and insurance purposes.
